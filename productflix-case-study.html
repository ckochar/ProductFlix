THE PROBLEM

A rich portfolio can become a navigation problem.

The site had professional work, AI thinking, prototypes, career history and personal stories. The risk was that a first-time visitor had to understand the information architecture before finding the evidence they cared about.

THE PRODUCT BET

Rank the same catalog by visitor intent.

01 · HIRING

Evidence first

Lead with shipped work, realized outcomes and career context.

02 · PRODUCT × AI

Trust & judgment first

Prioritize AI products, rules-vs-model decisions and human-in-the-loop thinking.

03 · BUILDING / LIFE

Different proof

Re-rank toward prototypes or personal stories without creating separate sites.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The important choices were behavioral, not visual.

“Personalization should reduce discovery cost, not create another gate.”
  • Ranking, not filtering: content stays discoverable in every mode.
  • No forced intro: the base URL opens directly in the Hiring cut.
  • URL beats memory: explicit ?mode= deep links always win.
  • Tap first: mode switching works without hover-only interactions.
  • Channel-aware: application links can open directly in the most relevant cut.
WHAT I’D MEASURE

Personalization is only useful if it changes behavior.

The portfolio became part of the portfolio.
