What would Chirag prioritize?

There isn't one universally correct path. My default is to separate deterministic certainty from probabilistic judgment, then spend human attention where risk is highest.

01Rules before model calls

Move high-confidence deterministic checks out of the LLM path first.

02Risk-tier human review

Keep humans on critical exceptions and low-confidence cases instead of reviewing everything.

03Route by task complexity

Use smaller models for simple extraction; reserve stronger models for ambiguous reasoning.

04Measure escapes, not vibes

Roll out behind evals, override telemetry and a feedback loop before relaxing controls further.
