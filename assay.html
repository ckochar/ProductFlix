PRODUCT DECISIONS

The interesting work was in the edges.

DECISION 01

Fail should block progression.

A critical failed rule cannot be visually equivalent to “needs review.” The workflow has to encode governance, not merely display model output.

DECISION 02

Evidence before trust.

Users need to see why a rule fired — not just a confidence score. The design therefore makes source evidence a first-class interaction.

DECISION 03

Human confirmation is a feature.

For regulated workflows, removing every click is not always the goal. A deliberate confirmation step creates accountability and a cleaner audit trail.

DECISION 04

Demo mode and live mode are different products.

The portfolio experience separates controlled sample data from actual analysis so visitors can explore safely without confusing synthetic output for production truth.

WHAT I LEARNED

Building changes the questions a PM asks.

01 · PRODUCT

States matter more than screens.

The hardest UX questions were not visual. They were about when a package can progress, when an exception blocks it, and how confidence maps to action.

02 · AI

Accuracy is only one metric.

Latency, evidence quality, false-positive behavior, review burden and failure transparency all shape whether the AI experience is useful.

03 · BUILDING

Prototype fidelity reveals strategy gaps.

Once the flow is interactive, vague product decisions become impossible to hide. Every ambiguous state eventually needs a concrete rule.

NEXT ITERATION

Where I would take Assay next.

“The next frontier is not better OCR. It is package-level intelligence, evidence quality and measurable trust.”
  • PDF evidence viewer — anchor each rule outcome directly to source evidence.
  • Rule profiles — configurable validation by product / package type.
  • Cross-document intelligence — identify contradictions between otherwise-valid documents.
  • Evaluation harness — track precision, recall, false positives and review burden.
  • Governance controls — explicit override, audit and approval logic.
THE POINT

Assay is not a second Doc Verify story.

It is proof that I can move from product strategy into hands-on prototyping, interaction design, deployment and iteration.
