01 · THE PROBLEM

High-volume QC was still a human search problem.

Loan-modification document packages flowed through a manual quality-control step before downstream processing. Auditors repeatedly searched for the same types of execution failures — missing borrower signatures, notary issues and incorrect dates — package after package.

The workflow worked, but it consumed skilled capacity on highly repetitive checks and pushed exceptions into a rework cycle that could take days.

02 · DISCOVERY

The breakthrough wasn't “use AI.” It was finding where AI was unnecessary.

Working with operations, I decomposed the QC workflow into repeatable failure modes. Roughly 80% of the review surface was rule-oriented, and most deterministic rejects clustered around a small set of checks.

That changed the solution architecture. Instead of asking an LLM to judge everything, we could reserve AI for document interpretation and ambiguity while keeping deterministic validation explicit and auditable.

04 · THE PRODUCT

AI reviews first. Humans review what matters.

Doc Verify creates a first-pass QC layer before manual review. It ingests a package, identifies relevant documents and execution evidence, runs deterministic checks, applies AI interpretation where needed, and routes exceptions with evidence for human confirmation.

05 · KEY PRODUCT DECISIONS

The product is defined by the boundaries.

01

Why not automate 100%?

In regulated workflows, false confidence can be more expensive than manual effort. Critical or ambiguous conditions remain reviewable by design.

02

Why rules before LLMs?

If a condition is deterministic, making it probabilistic adds cost and risk without adding product value.

03

Why evidence with every exception?

An auditor should not have to trust a model. They should see exactly what triggered the decision and where the relevant evidence lives.

04

Why phased rollout?

The rollout sequence was designed to learn from lower-complexity flows before expanding into more variable execution channels.

06 · ROLLOUT

Ship the learning loop, not the big bang.

The product was sequenced by workflow complexity: start with RON, then expand to QC-only packages, then mobile-notary flows. Each phase increases variation and exception complexity while preserving the same underlying verification model.

08 · WHAT I’D DO NEXT
01

Build an evaluation harness

Track precision, recall, critical false negatives and reviewer disagreement by rule and document type.

02

Turn overrides into learning

Use human corrections to identify weak rules, ambiguous layouts and model failure modes.

03

Add cross-document intelligence

Move from validating pages independently to identifying contradictions across the entire package.

04

Scale profiles by workflow

Make validation policies configurable by channel, client, document package and risk tolerance.
