## Remaining launch blockers / decisions
- Replace visual placeholders in photography, travel, sports, food and portrait pages with a curated set of real media.
- Add a downloadable resume once the final public resume file is selected.
- Decide whether to keep the CMU email address as the public contact or replace it with a long-term personal email.
- Final legal/confidentiality review of employer metrics and wording before public launch.
- Optional: replace Google Fonts with self-hosted/system fonts if you want fewer third-party requests.
- Optional: add analytics only after deciding what you actually want to measure.

## Recommended V1 success metrics
- Recruiter can identify Chirag’s core positioning within 30–60 seconds.
- At least one flagship case study is opened from the homepage.
- Visitors discover at least one “How I Think” experience.
- Contact / LinkedIn CTA remains visible without requiring a full-site read.
