THE PRODUCT PROBLEM

Clients should not need an internal operating manual to understand their work.

Mortgage servicing workflows span multiple operational steps, statuses and handoffs. When visibility lives across emails, status checks and disconnected touchpoints, even routine questions become expensive for clients and operations teams.

01 · VISIBILITY

One place to understand progress

Bring order and product status into a client-facing experience so users can see where work stands and what happens next.

02 · TRACKING

Make milestones meaningful

Translate internal workflow activity into client-readable milestones, status changes and product tracking.

03 · COORDINATION

Reduce status chasing

Design the portal so clients can answer common workflow questions without depending on fragmented communication.

MY PRODUCT ROLE

Build the client experience around the workflow, not around the org chart.

“The portal is successful when complex operations feel simple from the outside.”
  • Discovery and prioritization: translate client and operational pain points into a product roadmap.
  • Workflow design: define how internal statuses, milestones and product activity should appear to external users.
  • Cross-functional execution: align design, engineering, operations and business stakeholders around a shared client experience.
  • Platform thinking: create reusable patterns for tracking and visibility rather than one-off status screens.

WHY IT MATTERS

Enterprise UX is often an information-architecture problem disguised as a workflow problem.

The strongest client portals do more than expose internal data. They decide what matters, translate operational complexity into understandable states, and give users confidence about what is happening next.
