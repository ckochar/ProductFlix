# ProductFlix

An interactive product portfolio by **Chirag Kochar** — designed as a streaming-style experience across products, product thinking, hands-on builds, career chapters, and life beyond work.

## Portfolio structure

- **Work:** Title Automation, Doc Verify, Accounting & Payments, Travel Reservation, Digital Manufacturing / IIoT
- **Think:** Decision Simulator, AI Product Copilot, If I Were PM For…, Product Teardowns
- **Build:** Assay and experimental product work
- **Journey:** Career narrative, awards, certifications, and education
- **Life:** Photography, travel, sports, food, and portraits

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No build step is required.

## Deployment

Designed to deploy directly on Vercel as a static site.
