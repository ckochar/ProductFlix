WHAT I LEARNED

Enterprise product thinking started on the factory floor.

“The system is not successful when it deploys. It is successful when operations can trust it during a real shift.”
  • Configuration beats fragmentation: build common patterns with room for controlled variation.
  • Operations are users: workflows have to match how work actually happens, not how process maps say it happens.
  • Rollout is product strategy: sequencing, readiness and adoption can matter as much as feature scope.
  • Reliability is experiential: system uptime and data integrity shape user trust.
