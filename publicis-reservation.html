THE PRODUCT PROBLEM

A reservation is only as strong as its weakest state transition.

Travel looks simple to the customer: choose, pay, go. Behind the screen, availability, pricing, traveler details, payment authorization and confirmation can all change independently. The product challenge is keeping that complexity invisible without losing reliability.

01 · JOURNEY

Booking continuity

Preserve context as customers move from discovery into reservation and checkout.

02 · TRANSACTION

Payment reliability

Design for authorization outcomes, retries and failure recovery without confusing the traveler.

03 · TRUST

State clarity

Make it obvious what is reserved, what is paid and what still requires action.

WHAT I LEARNED

Product decisions become transaction decisions.

“A beautiful checkout is not enough. The real product is the state machine underneath it.”
  • Think end-to-end: optimize the reservation journey, not isolated screens.
  • Design failure states first: payments and availability create edge cases that become customer moments.
  • Integrations are product: dependencies shape latency, reliability and what users can trust.
  • Measure conversion with quality: successful booking matters more than button clicks.
